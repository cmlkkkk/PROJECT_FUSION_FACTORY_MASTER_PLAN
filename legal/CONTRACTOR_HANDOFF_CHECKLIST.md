# Contractor Handoff Checklist (template)

Not legal advice; have the agreement reviewed by qualified counsel. Paying a freelancer does not by itself
transfer all necessary rights.

- [ ] Written agreement signed before work starts
- [ ] Confidentiality
- [ ] Assignment of IP rights to the studio/company
- [ ] Warranty of no knowing third-party infringement
- [ ] Permission to modify and commercialize
- [ ] Payment terms
- [ ] Handling of third-party assets (provenance records required)
- [ ] Repository / security access: least privilege, 2FA, no shared credentials
- [ ] Termination and handover (access revoked, work delivered to Git)
